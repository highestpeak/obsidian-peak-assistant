Peak Assistant is a context-aware AI workspace for Obsidian. It helps you search, navigate, map, and maintain your knowledge — then carries context from your notes, activity, selected resources, projects, and the web into Composer, Chat Studio, and agents, so you can research, write, and act without starting over.

Context Intelligence

Keep the thread as you move.

Peak carries forward what you are working on, so moving from Search or Graph into Composer or Chat Studio does not mean rebuilding the context. One continuous working state, not a series of blank prompts.

current file, selection & block recent searches & opened material nodes and relations you're watching projects, conversations & history chosen files, PDFs, pages & resources active model, provider, tools & permissions

That continuity runs between the two places you spend the most time: the graph, where structure becomes something you can see and repair, and the source, where the change actually gets made.

01

Understand and maintain your knowledge.

Five surfaces read the same index from different angles: Vault Search finds material, AI Search researches a question, File Atlas opens a file as a map, Knowledge Maintenance watches the health of the whole vault, and Related Materials brings up what you did not think to ask for.

Vault Search

Find the material. See where it belongs.

One query — full-text, fuzzy, semantic, metadata. Reranked by your own graph.

Know what you want? Type keywords and operators — full-text, exact. Don't? Write plain language and hit Refine: Peak rewrites it into advanced query syntax for you.

No Peak plan required.

Peak Assistant has no subscription, seat, or usage fee, and it does not resell tokens. Connect your own providers and pay them directly — or run local models and pay nothing at all.

Your data never leaves.

Peak keeps no cloud copy of your workspace — no accounts, no telemetry on your notes. Everything stays plain files on your machine; only a call you explicitly make sends the context it needs to the provider you chose.
